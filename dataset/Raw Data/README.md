This supplementary data repository contains RNA-seq and clinical data from a prospective longitudinal cohort of 28 patients with sepsis enrolled under a University of Utah IRB-approved protocol with written informed consent. The cohort includes 18 patients previously reported by Wilson et al. (batch A: 10 with septic shock and 8 without) and 10 additional, previously unpublished patients (batch B: 2 with septic shock and 8 without).
The repository includes the original RNA-seq count data for batches A and B, together with the corresponding available clinical sample information. It also includes log2 fold-change values for sepsis versus recovered samples, calculated from the combined batch A and batch B dataset after trimmed mean of M-values (TMM) normalization.
Patients were enrolled within 48 hours of ICU admission, with peripheral blood collected at enrollment and again approximately 90 days after hospital discharge. Sepsis was defined using the SIRS-based consensus criteria in effect during recruitment, and septic shock was defined by the first administration of vasopressors during the ICU stay. Available clinical annotations include sepsis criteria, shock status, antiplatelet exposure, and microbiologic findings.
Platelets were isolated from peripheral blood, followed by RNA extraction and RNA sequencing. Sequencing reads were aligned to the reference genome, and gene-level expression counts were generated according to previously published methods. For downstream analyses combining batches A and B, count data were TMM-normalized, and log2 fold-change values comparing sepsis with the recovered state were calculated from the normalized expression data.
All samples were de-identified. Clinical annotation was limited by the approved protocol to sepsis criteria, shock status, antiplatelet exposure, and microbiology. Accordingly, additional sociodemographic and electronic health record variables, including age, sex, race, ethnicity, and comorbidities, were not included in the classifier analysis.
